# Code of Conduct

Jellypass is a small, friendly project. Everyone taking part, in issues, pull requests and discussions, is expected to treat others with respect.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. People have different levels of experience, and many are volunteering their time.
- Assume good intent, and say clearly what you need. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Keep to the topic, and keep private details (yours and other people's) out of public posts. Never paste API keys, tokens or personal data.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, insults, discrimination, threats, unwelcome personal attention, sharing other people's private information, and spam or promotion unrelated to the project.

## What happens

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close or lock conversations, and block people who break these rules, and will explain why where that is appropriate.

## Reporting a problem

If someone is not following this code, tell the maintainer privately by opening a [private report](https://github.com/Ol775/Jellypass/security/advisories/new) and starting the title with "Conduct", or use GitHub's **Report content** on the comment. Reports are treated confidentially.

This code applies in project spaces and when representing the project in public.
